Tournament Terms

  • The Thanksgiving Gauntlet runs November 20 to 27, 2026, inclusive, Eastern time.
  • Players are ranked by total amount staked on Onyx Mini Games during that window. Sweepstakes play only. Where two players are level, the one with more rounds ranks higher.
  • The top 50 places share 20,000 Bonus Onyx Cash, awarded as shown in the Prize column.
  • Prizes are credited within 48 hours of the completion of the tournament.
  • Standings refresh regularly through the tournament and are marked final once it closes.
