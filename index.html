Tournament Terms

  • The Mini Games Gauntlet runs November 12 to 18, 2026, inclusive, Eastern time.

  • The top 20 places share 10,000 Bonus Onyx Cash, awarded as shown in the Prize column.
  • Prizes are credited within 48 hours of the completion of the tournament.
  • Standings refresh regularly through the tournament and are marked final once it closes.
