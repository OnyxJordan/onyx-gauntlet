Tournament Terms

  • The Thanksgiving Gauntlet runs November 20 to 27, 2026, inclusive, Eastern time.

  • Prizes are credited within 48 hours of the completion of the tournament.
  • Standings refresh regularly through the tournament and are marked final once it closes.
